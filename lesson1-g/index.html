      Step 0718 min

      Exam training: write your own text

      Write about a time when you did something because you felt peer pressure. At least 80 words — write as much as you can.

      You don't have to write about yourself

      You can write about something that happened to a friend — or invent a story that never really happened. Nobody will ask which one it is.

      DEDu musst nicht über dich selbst schreiben. Es darf auch eine erfundene Geschichte sein.

      1

      Make notes

      Ideas: cyberbullying · tattoos · sport · smoking · clothes · phones · going somewhere you didn't want to go.

      Think of the WH-questions: When / Where did it happen? Who was there? Why did you do it?

      2

      The background

      Useful: This happened about two months ago. · I was at school / at home / … · I felt really happy / angry / jealous / …

      3

      How it started

      You can adapt a sentence from the article: One day a boy in my group had a new phone. It looked cool and soon everybody …

      4

      How the pressure worked, and how you felt

      Useful: I didn't want to … , but my friends said, “Are you frightened?” · I knew it was wrong, but … · I felt very frightened, but …

      Use speech marks to report what people said: My friends asked, “Do you want to …?”

      5

      How it ended

      Useful: Now I'm really happy that I … · Now I feel very sad / sorry / ashamed that …

      6

      Exchange texts with a partner

      Read your partner's text and give advice. Useful phrases:

      Something you liked

      This is great! · I like the part where … · Your ending is really strong.

      Something you didn't understand

      I don't understand this. What do you mean? · Who is “he” here?

      Advice

      You should write when this happened. · You could add what you said. · Maybe use speech marks here.

      7

      Revise

      • Is it at least 80 words? (see the counter below)
      • Did you write when and where it happened?
      • Did you use speech marks at least once?
      • Past tense for what happened?
      • Does the reader know how you felt at the end?

      Then put your finished text in your DOSSIER.
